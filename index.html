A little more about me.

I'm Akhil, a self-taught product designer with a background in Computer Science. An introvert and, apparently, a serious thinker.

I'm based in Hyderabad, though I spent a few years living in Bengaluru.

I've always been curious about how things work, why they're the way they are, and what could make them better. I enjoy questioning the obvious, exploring different possibilities, and experimenting with ideas just to see where they lead. Design eventually became a natural extension of that mindset. It gave me a way to turn those questions and ideas into something tangible. What started as an exploration gradually grew into something I genuinely enjoy doing.

Over the years, I've worked across fintech and HRMS, designing B2B and enterprise product experiences.
